The Side Quests

When I’m not wrangling data or wandering down an AI rabbit hole, I am usually reading a murder mystery novel or disappearing into a good sci-fi movie. I am also a lifelong Harry Potter nerd and will happily discuss character theories, plot details, and can make a fairly confident guess at your Hogwarts house.

Away from the screen, I’m usually behind a camera, collecting streets, people, and the small moments that make a place feel like itself. And then there is chai. Not an occasional cup, but a carefully brewed, completely unapologetic commitment.

I own more photographs than I will ever organize, have strong opinions about fictional universes, and firmly believe most problems improve after a properly brewed cup of chai.
