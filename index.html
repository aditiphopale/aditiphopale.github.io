The Side Quests

Beyond the dashboards, data models, and the occasional deep dive into whatever corner of AI has caught my attention, I am usually reading a murder mystery or watching something set in a future that is equal parts fascinating and mildly terrifying. I am also a lifelong Harry Potter nerd and will happily discuss character theories, plot details, and which Hogwarts house everyone secretly belongs in.

Away from the screen, I am almost always carrying a camera. When I travel, I am less interested in checking landmarks off a list and more interested in capturing how a place actually feels — its streets, people, routines, and the small moments that are easy to miss. And then there is chai. Not an occasional cup, but a carefully brewed, completely unapologetic commitment.

I own more photographs than I will ever organize, have strong opinions about fictional universes, and firmly believe most problems improve after a properly brewed cup of chai.
